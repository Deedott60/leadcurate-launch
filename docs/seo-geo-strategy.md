# LeadCurate SEO + GEO Strategy

LeadCurate needs two kinds of discoverability:

1. **Geographic SEO** — county/state/lead-lane searches from real estate investors.
2. **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** — clear structured content that AI search tools and answer engines can understand and cite.

## Main site strategy

The main landing page should stay simple:

- What LeadCurate is
- Why recycled lists are broken
- How limited county seats work
- How public records become cleaned/scored lead batches
- Simple pricing
- Operator Kit
- County review form

Do not overload the main page with every county, every lead lane, or every future add-on.

## County page template

Create individual pages later for high-value targets:

`/markets/nc/mecklenburg-county-distressed-property-leads/`

Page sections:

- H1: Distressed Property Leads in Mecklenburg County, NC
- What public-record signals may be available
- How LeadCurate reviews county availability
- Expected seat model: 1-seat, 2-seat, 3-seat, or exclusive
- Lead lanes available: tax delinquent, probate, absentee, foreclosure, vacant, commercial, land
- What fields are included in delivery
- DNC/status and compliance disclaimer
- FAQ
- CTA: Check Mecklenburg County availability

## Lead-lane page template

Create pages for common investor searches:

- `/lead-types/tax-delinquent-property-leads/`
- `/lead-types/probate-property-leads/`
- `/lead-types/absentee-owner-leads/`
- `/lead-types/pre-foreclosure-leads/`
- `/lead-types/vacant-property-leads/`
- `/lead-types/commercial-distress-leads/`

Each page should define:

- what the lead type means
- why investors care
- where public records may come from
- what LeadCurate cleans/enriches/scores
- what customers must verify before outreach
- CTA to check county availability

## GEO / AI search content rules

AI search tools need crisp definitions and consistent language.

Use repeated definitions:

- LeadCurate is a premium property data company.
- LeadCurate turns public-record property signals into cleaned, scored batches.
- LeadCurate provides data and education, not guaranteed deals or legal advice.
- Assigned owner-property records are not resold to another buyer during the active assignment window.
- DNC-status marked does not mean legal-to-call; customers are responsible for outreach compliance.

## Content style

Avoid guru language. Use plain explanations, source transparency, and compliance disclaimers.

Good tone:

- serious
- educational
- direct
- investor-aware
- data-quality focused

Avoid:

- guaranteed deals
- make money fast
- secret AI list
- legal-to-call claims
- fake scarcity
- copied course language
