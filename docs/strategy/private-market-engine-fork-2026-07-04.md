**Date:** 2026-07-04  
**Owner:** Derrick McDonald  
**Status:** Strategic fork, not a replacement for the original LeadCurate list business

1. **LeadCurate Private Market Engine**

4. **LeadCurate Private Intelligence Desk**

Current favorite direction: **LeadCurate Private Market Engine** for the customer-facing offer, with **LeadCurate OS** as the internal/platform name.

## One-sentence offer

LeadCurate installs and maintains a private lead intelligence system for a real-estate operator's chosen market, pulling public/source data on a schedule, cleaning it, scoring it, organizing it, and delivering fresh buyer-ready opportunities without the operator having to build the machine themselves.

## Why this is a fork

The original LeadCurate model sells curated motivated-seller lead lists and preview audits.

This fork sells the **system behind the lists** to higher-value buyers who want their own private pipeline:

- their market
- their lanes
- their schedule
- their scoring rules
- their reporting/dashboard
- their recurring data engine

This does **not** replace selling lists. The list business remains the front door. This fork becomes the premium upsell for serious operators.

## Core positioning

Do **not** pitch this as “we sell you an AI agent.” That sounds cheap or confusing.

Pitch the result:

> We build and maintain your private market intelligence engine. It monitors the records that matter, cleans the noise out, scores the opportunities, and delivers a prioritized lead workflow your team can actually work.

Behind the scenes, the system can use Hermes, Codex, n8n, Supabase, scrapers, scheduled jobs, browser automation, audit reports, and human QA. The customer buys the outcome, not the technical stack.

## What the customer gets

A Private Market Engine can include:

- dedicated market/county setup
- selected lead lanes, such as tax delinquent, probate, pre-foreclosure, code violations, permits, absentee, high equity, liens, vacant land
- scheduled public-record monitoring or recurring pulls
- cleaning, deduping, and residential/entity filtering
- scoring rules customized to the operator's strategy
- source attribution and pull dates
- recurring XLSX/CSV delivery
- dashboard or audit-style report
- optional CRM/n8n integration
- optional skip-trace integration if the client provides/pays for the skip-trace account
- monthly maintenance and troubleshooting

## What it is not

This is not a normal GPT wrapper.

It is not:

- a ChatGPT prompt pack
- a generic chatbot
- a $20/month AI assistant
- a scraped spreadsheet with no QA
- a guarantee of deals
- a skip-tracing product by default

The value is in the operational system: source access, scheduled pulls, parsing, scoring, suppression, QA, reporting, delivery, and maintenance.

## How to explain why this is not something their $20 GPT account can do

Simple version:

> A normal GPT account can answer questions. It cannot reliably log into county portals, pull records on a schedule, normalize messy files, dedupe parcels, score distress signals, maintain a database, generate audit reports, alert your team, and keep the workflow running when a source changes. We are not selling prompts. We are installing an operating system for your market data.

Short sales version:

> ChatGPT gives you answers. LeadCurate Private Market Engine gives you a working lead pipeline.

More premium version:

> This is infrastructure, not a conversation. The value is the maintained workflow that turns messy public records into a clean, scored, recurring market feed.

## Product ladder

### 1. LeadCurate Preview Audit
Purpose: proof and lead generation.

- free or low-cost
- shows market opportunity
- redacted sample records/charts
- CTA into a curated list or private system consultation

### 2. Curated List
Purpose: first paid proof.

- one-time list
- $149 to $1,497 depending on tier/current LeadCurate model
- proves quality of data, scoring, and packaging

### 3. Monthly Refresh
Purpose: subscription bridge.

- recurring list refresh
- $250 to $1,500/month depending on market/lane/exclusivity
- easiest upgrade from one-time list

### 4. Private Market Engine
Purpose: premium custom system.

- setup fee plus monthly maintenance
- customer gets their own configured market workflow
- this is the fork described in this document

## Pricing hypotheses

### Starter Private Engine
For a single county, one lane, simple monthly delivery.

- **Setup:** $997 to $2,500
- **Monthly:** $497 to $750
- Includes: one county, one lane, monthly pull, cleaned XLSX, basic scoring, simple audit summary

### Private Market Engine
For serious local operators.

- **Setup:** $2,500 to $5,000
- **Monthly:** $750 to $1,500
- Includes: one market, 2-4 lead lanes, recurring refresh, scoring, report/dashboard, support, source logic, QA

### Multi-Market Intelligence Desk
For acquisition teams, funds, or high-volume operators.

- **Setup:** $7,500 to $15,000+
- **Monthly:** $2,500 to $5,000+
- Includes: multiple markets, multiple lanes, custom scoring, dashboard, CRM/n8n integration, priority support, private workflows

## Buyer profile

Best buyers:

- established wholesalers
- acquisitions teams
- local investor groups
- real estate funds
- land buyers
- probate/inherited-property specialists
- high-volume flippers
- operators already paying for data tools but still drowning in noise

Weak buyers:

- beginners with no budget
- people only asking for cheap skip tracing
- people who do not work leads consistently
- people expecting AI magic without operational discipline

## Why this could be stronger than just selling lists

Selling lists creates one-time revenue and proves the data.

Selling the engine creates:

- higher setup fees
- monthly recurring revenue
- deeper client lock-in
- a premium positioning moat
- a reason to keep improving the backend
- less commodity comparison against PropStream/ListSource

The strategic insight: **LeadCurate can sell the list first, then sell the machine to buyers who want the private version.**
