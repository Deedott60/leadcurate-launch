# LeadCurate Commercial Storyboard v3 — Reference-First Reset

## Goal
Create a realistic, calm, trust-first LeadCurate commercial inspired by the reference insurance-style ad: human-first, soft office realism, measured pacing, restrained captions, credible product reveal, clean end card.

This is the corrected workflow: script + storyboard + still frames first, then video generation only after the still board is approved.

## Brand position
LeadCurate helps serious real estate investors stop working the same stale property lists everyone else has already touched. It reviews county source records, cleans and dedupes them, prioritizes signals, and reserves limited-seat batches with source context.

## Style rules
- Human-first, not dashboard-first.
- Calm, serious, credible, not flashy.
- Medium / medium-close framing.
- 50mm lens feel, shallow depth of field.
- Soft daylight through office windows.
- Cream, slate/navy, muted emerald palette.
- Minimal captions: one short sentence at a time.
- No fake UI text generated inside AI footage.
- Any product/dashboard text should be added in post, not generated in the image/video model.

## Visual storyboard

### Frame 1 — Human problem recognition
**Time:** 0:00–0:05  
**Visual:** Sarah Thompson, professional woman in her early 40s, sitting in a quiet office at a desk. She reviews printed property records and looks concerned but composed. No dramatic acting.  
**Camera:** Medium-close, 50mm, shallow focus, slow push-in.  
**Caption:** "Another list is not the advantage."

### Frame 2 — The stale-list problem
**Time:** 0:05–0:10  
**Visual:** Over-the-shoulder shot of Sarah comparing messy property records, duplicate-looking rows, and a muted laptop/tablet with abstract blurred rows. No readable fake text.  
**Camera:** Over-the-shoulder, soft focus, slight parallax.  
**Caption:** "Source, timing, and context matter."

### Frame 3 — LeadCurate process appears
**Time:** 0:10–0:16  
**Visual:** Clean premium desk scene: county folders, tablet, subtle abstract dashboard blocks. Human hands organize records into neat stacks. No readable generated UI.  
**Camera:** Controlled tabletop shot, slow lateral slide.  
**Caption:** "Review. Clean. Prioritize. Reserve."

### Frame 4 — Advisor / buyer confidence
**Time:** 0:16–0:22  
**Visual:** Sarah on a calm call or reviewing notes with a serious investor/buyer off-camera. She is confident and measured, not salesy.  
**Camera:** Medium shot, warm office daylight, natural face movement.  
**Caption:** "Work the county with better context."

### Frame 5 — Premium product proof
**Time:** 0:22–0:27  
**Visual:** Clean, non-literal LeadCurate-style dashboard mockup added in post: limited seats, county review date, source context, score reason. Keep design cream/navy/emerald.  
**Camera:** Static product proof with subtle push.  
**Caption:** "Limited-seat county property data."

### Frame 6 — End card
**Time:** 0:27–0:30  
**Visual:** Cream background, LeadCurate wordmark, restrained CTA.  
**Text:** "LeadCurate" / "Check county availability"  
**Tone:** institutional, confident, not hypey.
