Privacy Policy

Dollar Leads, a LeadCurate company · Last updated July 19, 2026

What we collect from you

When you place an order we collect your name, email address, and your order details (county, category, pack, and any notes you add). If you email us, we keep that correspondence.

How we use it

  • To confirm your order, send your payment link, and deliver your file.
  • To answer your questions and handle any fulfillment issue.
  • To keep basic order history so batch seat limits work as promised.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with anyone.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you bought from us or asked us to, and you can opt out any time by replying.
  • We do not store card numbers; payment happens inside your payment app, not on this site.

About the property data we sell

The property records in our products come from official public government sources, not from tracking individuals. Owner names and addresses in those files are public record as published by the counties.

Data retention and your rights

Order records are kept for business and tax purposes. You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it (where the law does not require us to keep it), by emailing hello@leadcurate.com.
