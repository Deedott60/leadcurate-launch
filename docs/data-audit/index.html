LeadCurate sources directly from official county portals — not licensed reseller feeds. That gives two structural advantages over PropStream / BatchLeads / DealMachine: freshness (county data hits LeadCurate within hours of being published, vs 30–90 days for the licensors) and scarcity by design (limited seats per county per lane, a model competitors structurally cannot offer).

Markets covered

Three tiers, organized by demand × accessibility.

Tier S — flagship

Mecklenburg NC · Wake NC · Tarrant TX · Harris TX · Maricopa AZ · Marion IN

High investor demand, large addressable data, and clean automation. Mecklenburg is the local pilot. Tarrant's weekly tax-roll zip (2 million records) is the best free data source in the country. Marion IN was ranked #1 wholesale market for 2026.

Tier A — strong secondary

Cuyahoga OH · Jefferson KY · Fulton GA · DeKalb GA · Forsyth NC · NYC · Dallas TX · Shelby TN · Guilford NC

Solid wholesale markets, downloadable raw data on disk. Shelby TN is the scarcity asset — the source CSV URL is published but most competitors don't know it exists.

Tier B — supplementary

Allen IN · Erie NY · Charleston SC · Greenville SC · Fayette KY

Smaller markets or PDF-format publications. Specialty buyers care about these even when bigger competitors ignore them.

Compliance posture

Built into every product.

  • No skip-trace contact data in entry/mid tiers — zero TCPA exposure, 100% margin on data delivery.
  • Source URL + pull date stamped in metadata of every product. Buyer verifies our claims directly.
  • Compliance note in every metadata file: "Property-record data only. Buyer is responsible for owner contact lookup, skip trace, DNC compliance, TCPA, and outreach."
  • No CAPTCHA bypass, no anti-bot evasion, no paywall scraping. Every source is an official portal, open-data hub, or legally required publication.
  • Assignment lock — records assigned to one customer during their billing cycle are held back from other buyers' batches.
