Distress lanes

Eleven categories, matching the intake form exactly.

A lane is a category of distress signal that defines what kind of buyer the record is worth selling to. The same county can support multiple lanes simultaneously, each serving a different buyer profile without overlap.

#LaneWhat it identifies
1Tax DelinquentOverdue property tax, in-state or out-of-state owner
2Probate / InheritedDecedent-estate ownership, heirs deciding what to do with the property
3Pre-ForeclosureActive foreclosure case with filing and sale dates
4Code ViolationsActive code-enforcement actions
5LiensMechanic, judgment, or municipal liens on the property
6Absentee OwnerMailing address differs from property address
7Active Permits / DamageOpen building permits signaling active repair or distress
8High-Equity / Free-and-ClearLow or no mortgage balance relative to value
9Individual / HomeownerOwner-occupied, real person living at the property
10Entity-Owned (LLC)Corporate or investor-owned property
11Vacant LandUndeveloped lots with an identifiable owner

Not every lane is available in every market yet. Tell us your market and lane on the intake form and we'll confirm what's ready.

Freshness

Why our data lands earlier than the platform tools.

The major industry tools — PropStream, BatchLeads, DealMachine — license their property data from upstream wholesalers like CoreLogic, ATTOM, and DataTree. Those feeds refresh on cycles of thirty to ninety days. A record showing as tax-delinquent in PropStream today may belong to an owner who paid their bill forty-five days ago. The signal is stale.

"A name that hits the public record on the first of the month is in our system within days. The same name does not appear in PropStream's data for another 30 to 90 days." The freshness story in one sentence

The practical effect: we are routinely 30 to 60 days ahead of the platform-based competitors on the same names.

Compliance posture

Built into every record.

  • Property-record data only. No skip-traced phone numbers, no email addresses, no DNC scrub in the entry and mid tiers. That is deliberate.
  • Every batch carries the source URL and source pull date. Buyers can verify our claims directly against the county portal we drew from.
  • We do not scrape behind paywalls or anti-bot controls. Every source is an official public-record portal, an open-data hub, or a legally required publication.
  • Buyers handle their own outreach, skip trace, DNC compliance, and TCPA decisions. LeadCurate provides data and educational tools only and does not guarantee deals.

What's next

The path from here.

Three counties remain to bring online (Harris TX, Cobb GA, Jefferson AL). Each requires browser-driven extraction rather than direct download — a focused work session each, not a fundamental obstacle.

Beyond data coverage, the next investments are around automation (recurring monthly pulls without human triggering), enrichment (deeper cross-referencing across datasets within a county), and customer-facing delivery infrastructure (turning the existing delivery format into a recurring subscription with payment automation).

The data foundation is solid. The path from here is execution.
