LEADCURATE — BUSINESS BRIEF WHAT IS LEADCURATE? A data service that pulls property records directly from county tax and court sources, scores them for owner motivation, and sells curated motivated-seller lists to wholesalers, fix-and-flip investors, and buy-and-hold landlords. Competitors: PropStream, BatchLeads, ListSource, DealMachine, PropertyRadar. Founder: Derrick McDonald. Solo operator, Phase 1 launch. LeadCurate tracks data velocity and trigger urgency — when distress is accelerating, when an auction date is scheduled, when a fresh filing hits the court. Same raw data, scored for WHEN motivation peaks. We also cap buyer access per county to 1-3 seats so records stay warm.
