The framework

Same data, four shelves.

Every record in the inventory gets tagged into one or more of four urgency tiers, scored on time-to-event rather than just static distress. This is the difference between PropStream (static lists) and us (live trackers of human motivation).

⚠ Internal only — customer doesn't see this yet

The 4-tier system is the back-end scoring framework. For Phase 1 launch, customers still see source-based products (Tax Delinquent, Absentee, etc.). The tier tags are visible only on the dashboard and quote sheets, used to recommend bundles and price premium add-ons. Full tier visibility unlocks at Phase 3 launch.

The pitch

How to explain this to a wholesaler (use these words).

The 30-second velocity pitch

"We don't just look at how long a property has been on a list — we track its velocity. Someone owing $500 for three years isn't motivated. But our system flags the exact moment that debt hits a breaking point — like when penalties stack into thousands, or when the county officially schedules an auction date. We catch the trigger event when the heat gets turned up. You aren't calling someone who is casually behind on taxes — you're calling someone who realizes they're about to lose their house to the county next month. That's why our data closes deals while standard software lists just sit there."

Internal note: Lead with this pitch in cold outreach DMs. The velocity framing differentiates us from every PropStream / BatchLeads competitor in the prospect's head, before they even ask about price.
