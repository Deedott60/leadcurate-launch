What's in every record

This is not just a name on a spreadsheet.

Every record comes from official county files and carries everything the county publishes. For an out-of-state owner, that means their name, the property here, AND the mailing address where they actually live.

●

Owner name

The current owner of record, plus business or co-owner names where the county lists them.

●

Property address

The full property location, with city, ZIP, and neighborhood or township detail where published.

●

Owner mailing address

Where the owner really gets mail, kept separate from the property. This is how out-of-state and absentee owners are proven, not guessed.

●

Official parcel number

The county's own ID for the property, so you can verify any record yourself in seconds.

Values and property type

County land, building, and total values where published, plus the official property class.

How long they've owned it

Last sale date and years held where the county shows it. A 20-year hold is a different conversation than a 2-year hold.

Straight answers

What you get and what you don't.

What's in each record?

Owner name, property address, owner mailing address, official parcel number, property type, county values where published, how long the owner has held it where the county shows it, and the reason it made the list.

Is this skip traced?

No. No phone numbers, no emails. This is county-record data you run through your own skip tracer or dialer. That keeps packs at these prices.

Is it scrubbed against the Do Not Call list?

No. You are responsible for your own outreach compliance, including DNC and TCPA. We sell the data as compiled from public county records.

Can I use this to screen tenants or run credit checks?

No. This is public property data, not a consumer report, and it cannot be used for credit, employment, tenant screening, or insurance decisions. Those uses fall under the Fair Credit Reporting Act and require a consumer reporting agency, which we are not. Use this for property research and owner outreach.

Which records do I actually get? Is it luck of the draw?

Not luck. Every county and category is split into numbered files, and paid orders are assigned the next file in line, in order. First buyers get the front of the inventory. Your file's number is printed on it, and once a file has been assigned its records are tracked so they are not handed out over and over. You are buying a reserved slice, not a raffle ticket.

Will other people have my exact list?

No public recycled list. Each paid file is assigned from tracked inventory, and different files inside the same county and category do not share records.

How fresh is it really?

Every county reruns against current official records each monthly cycle, and the cycle date is printed on your file. On days we run a same-morning scrub, a Fresh Scrub pack appears for the counties we scrubbed, and only on those days.

Can categories overlap?

Files inside the same county and category do not share records. A property can still qualify for two different categories, such as long-hold and out-of-state owner, so separate category orders can contain the same parcel. That overlap is a real multi-signal match, not a duplicate inside either file.

Can I pick the category?

Yes. Pick your county on the order form and it shows only the available categories for that market. Each order is one category from one county, so your file stays clean. Want two categories? Place two orders.

Do you have probate, inherited property, foreclosure, or liens?

Only when that category appears on the live board for the county you select. We add these lanes county by county when current official records support them. We never label a property probate, inherited, foreclosed, or liened from an unsupported proxy.

What if I need way more than 500 records?

That's our parent company's lane. LeadCurate builds full custom territory databases for serious operators. Ask and we'll connect you.
