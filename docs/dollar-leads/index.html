Straight answers

What you get and what you don't.

What's in each record?

Owner name, property address, owner mailing address, official parcel number, property type, county values where published, how long the owner has held it where the county shows it, and the reason it made the list.

Is this skip traced?

No. No phone numbers, no emails. This is county-record data you run through your own skip tracer or dialer. That keeps packs at these prices.

Is it scrubbed against the Do Not Call list?

No. You are responsible for your own outreach compliance, including DNC and TCPA. We sell the data as compiled from public county records.

How fresh is it really?

The pull date is printed on your file, so you never have to take our word for it. Fresh Drops are pulled or re-checked the same week you order.

Can I pick the category?

Yes. When you reserve, tell us which list you want from that county's board: long-hold landlords, out-of-state owners, tax-debt owners, or vacant land, wherever offered.

What if I need way more than 500 records?

That's our parent company's lane. LeadCurate builds full custom territory databases for serious operators. Ask and we'll connect you.
