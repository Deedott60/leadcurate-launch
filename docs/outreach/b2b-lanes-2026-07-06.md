## Lane 2 — Restoration / facade / masonry / mechanical contractors (NYC)

**Who:** facade restoration companies (FISP/LL11 work), masonry contractors, boiler service companies, fire/water remediation firms. Find them: NYC contractor license lookup (DOB-licensed facade contractors are a public list), LinkedIn, Google Maps "facade restoration NYC", trade associations (BTEA, Building Restoration trade groups).

**Why they buy:** an active DOB violation is an owner legally required to spend money, with fines accruing. Facade violations REQUIRE a licensed contractor. This isn't lead-gen — it's a list of buildings that must hire someone in their trade.

**Email/DM script (subject: "54,513 NYC buildings with open violations in your trade"):**

>
> One record verbatim: "EMERGENCY WORK ORDER DUE TO THE IMMEDIATE HAZARD POSED BY THE CRACKED AND SPALLING CONCRETE AND MASONRY STUCCO AT ALL EXPOSURES."
>
> Sample here (house numbers redacted): [NYC Violations link]
>
> We can cut it by borough and by violation class — facade-only in Manhattan, hazardous in Brooklyn, whatever your crew works. Want the count for your trade + territory?

**Voice-note talking points:** "Every building on this list has an open violation the owner is legally required to fix — facade jobs literally require a contractor like you. We rank them by how loaded they are. Tell me your borough and trade, I'll tell you how many buildings are waiting."

**Suggested pricing (Derrick locks):** $149–249 per borough+class cut (e.g. "Manhattan facade") · $499 citywide single class · refresh monthly as subscription. Smaller tickets than legal, but many more buyers.
