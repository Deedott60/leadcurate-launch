# LeadCurate commercial storyboard draft

Reference direction: calm, realistic, human-led commercial. Not a website walkthrough. Premium, trustworthy, practical.

## Target feel

- Real person, not flashy AI motion
- Female professional as the guide: acquisitions analyst, lead manager, or investor operations advisor
- Warm office / desk / tablet environment
- Calm voiceover
- Story first, product second
- LeadCurate as the trusted filter between messy public records and usable lead batches

## 30-second structure

### 0–4s — The problem

Visual: Female professional at a desk reviewing a messy spreadsheet / county records / property tabs on a laptop.

Mood: Focused, slightly frustrated, but not dramatic.

Voiceover idea:

> Every investor wants better leads. But most are still working the same tired lists.

On-screen text:

> Same lists. Same calls. Same problem.

### 4–8s — The hidden issue

Visual: Close shots of crossed-out duplicate rows, stale dates, mismatched owner names, confusing county record screens.

Voiceover:

> The problem usually is not the script. It is the records behind it.

On-screen text:

> Stale records. Duplicates. Weak context.

### 8–14s — LeadCurate enters

Visual: Female professional opens a clean LeadCurate batch on a tablet. The interface feels branded: cream, emerald, dark slate.

Voiceover:

> LeadCurate reviews county records, refines the batch, and adds the context your team needs before outreach starts.

On-screen text:

> Source. Refine. Prioritize. Reserve.

### 14–20s — What customers receive

Visual: Clean batch view with blurred/sample owners, lead category, source date, score, DNC/contact status, assignment ID.

Voiceover:

> Each batch is organized by opportunity type, source date, score, and assignment status.

On-screen text:

> Cleaner batches your team can act on.

### 20–25s — Limited-seat positioning

Visual: Map/county view with only 1–3 buyer seats shown. Calm emerald highlight around selected territory.

Voiceover:

> And because each county is reviewed first, access stays limited to what the market can actually support.

On-screen text:

> Reviewed counties. Limited seats.

### 25–30s — Brand close

Visual: Female professional closes tablet or looks at screen with confidence. Fade to LeadCurate brand card.

Voiceover:

> LeadCurate. Property leads worth working.

On-screen text:

> LeadCurate
> Limited-seat property owner leads
> Check My County

## Character reference direction

Female professional, early 30s to early 40s. Confident, composed, trustworthy. She should feel like someone who understands real estate operations, not a stock-photo influencer.

Wardrobe:

- neutral blazer or structured cardigan
- cream, black, slate, or muted green accents
- no loud colors
- no luxury flex / guru energy

Setting:

- modern office
- desk with laptop/tablet
- subtle papers/maps/property records
- warm natural light
- realistic but polished
