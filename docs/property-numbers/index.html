Three markets that punch above their weight

The less-obvious leverage in the inventory.

The big names — Maricopa, NYC, the Texas metros — speak for themselves. These are the markets a casual reviewer would overlook that carry serious analytical advantage.

#1 wholesale market in America right now

Marion County, IN — Indianapolis

Ranked the #1 US wholesale market for 2026 by multiple industry sources, yet it sits below Phoenix and DFW in casual visibility. The data: 347,143 parcels with owner detail and assessed values, 29,490 identified absentee residential owners.

The inflow of absentee capital is structurally different from Charlotte's. Top mailing states feeding Indianapolis: California (5,683), Georgia (2,785), Arizona (2,543), Texas (2,174), Ohio (2,128).

Takeaway: California sends nearly twice as much absentee capital to Indianapolis as Arizona does — the opposite of Charlotte's pattern, where Arizona dominates. A wholesaler can use this directional intel to pick the right outreach state per metro.

The largest sales-comp dataset we hold. 130,093 recent parcel sales 2021 through present — four years of transaction history on 527,160 parcels. Deeper than what's available in most Sun Belt markets where transaction-level history is sparser in public-record form.

Method

How these numbers were derived.

  • Residential = parcel records where Property_Use contains "single-family," "townhouse," or "condo."
  • Absentee = owner mailing-address state ≠ property state.
  • High-value = $200,000+ total assessed value. County-published assessed values typically run 10–30% below market.
  • NYC, Marion, Mecklenburg, Wake, Cuyahoga, Jefferson KY, Forsyth, Fulton, DeKalb, Guilford, Fayette, and Shelby counts are exact from CSV files.
  • Maricopa, Tarrant, and Dallas counts are estimated from binary tax-roll files pending full ingestion. Actuals likely to revise upward.
