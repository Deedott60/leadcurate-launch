What this tells a buyer

Pick the market that matches the strategy.

A wholesaler chasing high-volume cash-buyer flips picks Cleveland — 77% of its parcels sit under $100k, the deepest affordable-housing inventory in the audit. Why this is the largest unrealized lane

Mecklenburg alone has 311,139 individual residential owners.

Every Discovery Snapshot in production right now is sliced by distress signal (tax delinquent, pre-foreclosure, code violations, vacant land, liens). The Individual Owner Lane is a different axis — it cuts across all distress signals and isolates the records where the wholesaler is dealing with a real human, not a corporate entity. Combined with distress filtering, it produces the highest-converting wholesale targets. A "tax-delinquent individual owner-occupant" record is meaningfully more actionable than "tax-delinquent record" alone.

Method

How these numbers were derived.

  • Residential = parcel records where Property_Use contains "single-family," "townhouse," or "condo."
  • Absentee = owner mailing-address state ≠ property state.
  • High-value = $200,000+ total assessed value. County-published assessed values typically run 10–30% below market.
  • NYC, Marion, Mecklenburg, Wake, Cuyahoga, Jefferson KY, Forsyth, Fulton, DeKalb, Guilford, Fayette, and Shelby counts are exact from CSV files.
  • Maricopa, Tarrant, and Dallas counts are estimated from binary tax-roll files pending full ingestion. Actuals likely to revise upward.
  • Structured records = rows counted directly from each market's primary parcel file. Auxiliary detail tables (building fixtures, exemption codes, structural elements) describe existing parcels rather than additional properties and are not counted separately.
