The evidence-backed seventh lane

Detroit blight pressure adds a signal the six standard lanes miss.

The City of Detroit publishes a daily blight-ticket service. Restricting it to responsible dispositions, positive balances, and parcel-linked records produces a current municipal-pressure lane that can be joined directly to ownership, mailing, value, and property facts.
