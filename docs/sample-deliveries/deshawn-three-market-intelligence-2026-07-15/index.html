Verified vacant land

Vacant should mean vacant when the campaign starts.

Static land lists become unreliable as parcels change. Our vacant-land delivery is rebuilt against the current official parcel source before it ships. The result is a cleaner outreach pool with fewer parcels that are already improved or were never truly vacant.

Refresh speed

How quickly can new data reach your lanes?

The market mappings and lane logic already exist. That means a newly published supported parcel file does not require rebuilding the research process from the beginning.

01AcquireRetrieve the new official county or state source.
02NormalizeApply the saved market mapping and preserve useful source fields.
03RecomputeRebuild lane flags, tenure, ownership location, and signal crosses.
04VerifyDeduplicate by parcel and compare file counts with metadata.
05ReleaseProduce the audit update and the approved working file.
Current operating target: once a supported official parcel source is retrieved, the saved configuration can normally be rebuilt and manually checked within one business day. This is not a claim that every government source publishes daily. Pre-foreclosure and tax-lien timing is confirmed separately because those lanes depend on local event access.
