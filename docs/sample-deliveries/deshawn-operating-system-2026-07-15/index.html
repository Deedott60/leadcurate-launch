The operating system

The advantage is the chain, not a secret county website.

Other investors can work these markets. The difference is having a repeatable machine that turns changing public records into a private, prioritized buying queue.

1. SourceGo to the official county or state record, not a recycled reseller list.
2. NormalizePreserve useful source fields and standardize parcels across different systems.
3. DeduplicateOne row per parcel so counts and campaign size are real.
4. Cross signalsTenure plus absentee. Property type plus an off-market signal.
5. PrioritizeMove the smallest, strongest cut to the front of the working order.
6. VerifyKeep parcel identifiers and source context attached for official lookup.
7. RerunUse the saved market configuration when the public source changes.

What exclusivity actually means

The public records are not exclusive, and no honest provider should pretend they are. Geographic or lane exclusivity is a commercial term that must be written into an agreement and priced separately.

  • Standard service: private deliverables, no market lockout
  • Protected service: defined county, lane, and time window
  • Full exclusivity: higher retainer because LeadCurate turns away other work
