Compliance posture

  • 100% primary public records — county tax assessor, city lien filings, register of deeds. No credit bureau data, no consumer report data.
  • Property-record data only: no SSNs, no dates of birth, no bank information, no skip-traced phone numbers.
  • Provided for post-judgment asset identification and enforcement of existing legal claims — not for use in determining eligibility for credit, employment, insurance, or housing, and not usable as a consumer report under the FCRA.
  • Every record carries its source URL and pull date. Verify any row directly against the county portal before you rely on it.
