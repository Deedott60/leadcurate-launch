Your lanes, broken down

Four of your six lanes are verified and ready in Dallas today.

Tired Landlords

42,092 non-owner-occupied properties held 10+ years. Median hold: 16.8 years.

These are owners who bought when prices were a fraction of today's and have been collecting rent or sitting on the asset ever since. Median property value in this segment is $246,055. 6,128 of these owners live outside Texas entirely, and 4,722 of the properties are multifamily. The top of this file reaches institutional scale, including a 324-unit apartment property valued at $231M, held by a long-tenure non-occupant owner.

Industrial / Multifamily Distress

18,504 industrial and multifamily properties carrying an off-market signal.

16,182 multifamily and 2,322 industrial parcels where the owner either does not occupy the property or has held it 10+ years, the two conditions that precede quiet, off-market sales. Median value $358,820, median hold 11.5 years, and 13,593 of these owners are absentee. This is your core lane, and Dallas has real depth in it.

Out-of-State Owners

31,794 Dallas County properties owned from another state.

Median value $292,205. Notably, the median hold here is only 6.4 years, meaning much of this segment bought recently at higher prices. Cross-referencing this against the tired-landlord file (long tenure) separates motivated legacy owners from recent institutional buyers, and your delivered file carries the fields to do exactly that.

Verified Vacant Land

36,870 parcels verified as genuinely vacant. Median land value $75,600.

Every parcel cleared the same verification standard we run in every market: no hidden building value, county classification supports vacant, real usable acreage, an owner who can actually sell. 23,300 of these are absentee-owned, and 1,441 are industrially zoned vacant land, a direct play for your industrial interest.

Pre-foreclosure and tax-delinquent lists are not published inside the county appraisal roll this audit was built from. Both are sourced separately in Texas (trustee sale postings and tax office records) and get scoped per order rather than promised here. Everything above is verified and deliverable today.

Executive insights

What the numbers actually say.

Dallas is a multifamily off-market machine. 16,182 of the 18,504 distress-signal properties are multifamily. If your crew can execute on small and mid-size apartment product, this county's bench is deep.
Tenure separates the sellers from the holders. The out-of-state file skews recent (6.4-year median hold) while the tired-landlord file skews legacy (16.8 years). The overlap, out-of-state AND long-tenure, is the sharpest list in the delivery: 6,128 owners.
Vacant land here is not scraps. A $75,600 median land value across 36,870 verified parcels means this is buildable, sellable dirt, and 63% of it is absentee-owned.
