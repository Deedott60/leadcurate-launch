How we built this

  • Started from 1,164 raw parcels the county flagged with a probate/deed keyword match.
  • Stripped LLCs, trusts, and corporate owners - 1,063 of those records were entities, not real decedent estates.
  • Stripped businesses with "Real Estate" in the name (investment firms, U-Haul, Wal-Mart's real estate holding entity) that the keyword match falsely caught.
  • Kept only genuine "[Name] Estate" / "Estate of [Name]" records - real decedent-owned residential property, 72 parcels.
  • Absentee flag = heir's mailing address is outside Charlotte - often the easiest conversations, since the heir isn't emotionally tied to the property day-to-day.
