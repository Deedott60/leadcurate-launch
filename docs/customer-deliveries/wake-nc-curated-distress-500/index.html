How to work this list

  • HOT records (196): biggest debt or most years behind. Real motivation. Call this week.
  • WARM records (304): substantial distress, less acute. Mix of calls + direct mail.
  • Absentee owners (156): mailing address is in a different ZIP than the property. Higher conversion rate - owner can't easily check on the property and is often emotionally detached.
  • Estimated equity = property value minus tax debt. Your negotiation room - what the owner could net at close.
  • Years behind counts unique unpaid tax years. 3+ years = serious distress signal.
  • Skip-trace through your own provider for phone numbers. We deliver address + owner only, keeping you TCPA-safe by default.
