## Posture that's still valid

The launch posture remains:

- **Quality-controlled data operation first**, full SaaS dashboard later.
- Preserve raw imports, decision context, exclusion reasons, suppression rules, audit trail.
- One pilot county and one controlled batch before broad automation.
- Manual-assisted MVP before full platform.
- Founder-led sales before broad ads.
- No guaranteed-deal language. No "safe to call." No outcome guarantees.
- Customer must verify claims before outreach.
