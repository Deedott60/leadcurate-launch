Outlier worth noting

Houston Permit Burnout is the highest-converting product in the inventory.

1,500 records, but 88% above $100K and 52% absentee. The combination of damage permits + out-of-area owners is the strongest dual-signal distress pattern in real estate data. These records command premium pricing.

What this tells you: raw volume is not the value driver — signal density is. A 1,500-record list with two stacked distress signals outsells a 50,000-record dumped county pull. Margin lives in the filter stack.

Cross-market read

Why this number matters

Absentee + delinquent is the highest-converting combo in the industry.

Industry-wide, absentee delinquent properties convert to deals 3–4× more often than owner-occupied ones. The owner doesn't live there, isn't emotionally attached, and is paying taxes on something they don't use. They're a phone call away from selling.

Translation: a 5,000-record list filtered to absentee-only is more valuable than a 50,000-record general delinquent dump. Filter density beats raw volume on every meaningful metric.

Strategic alignment
