## 1. Purpose

LeadCurate will configure a hosted, white-label real estate consulting system for The
3 CCC'S Consulting Firm LLC. The system is intended to combine a customer-facing
website, an investor intake path, the Property Decision Tool, and controlled access to
approved property-data markets.

The system is decision support. It does not guarantee property availability, seller
motivation, contactability, deal profitability, or investment results.

## 2. Proposed Phase 1

Phase 1 includes the following, subject to the acceptance gates in Section 8:

1. **White-label consulting website**
   - Client business name, approved logo, colors, domain, phone, email, and service
     territory configured from one client profile.
   - Separate paths for property owners and real estate investors.
   - Client-approved intake questions and routing destination.
   - Standard privacy, terms, outreach-transparency, and opt-out pages.

2. **White-label Property Decision Tool**
   - Client-branded workspace.
   - Property search, record detail, map, comparable-record view, CSV import, and
     Flip, Rental, BRRRR, and Wholesale analysis.
   - Printable decision-support report.
   - Sample data remains in place until an approved market passes the data gate and
     Derrick authorizes its connection.

3. **Four-market data foundation**
   - Wayne County / Detroit, Michigan.
   - Mecklenburg County / Charlotte, North Carolina.
   - Wake County / Raleigh, North Carolina.
   - Guilford County / Greensboro and High Point, North Carolina.
   - Available lanes are determined separately for each market from the freshest
     official sources. A lane is not included merely because it exists in another
     market.
   - Customer access is limited to lanes that pass the current LeadCurate quality
     gate on the exact files made available.

4. **Initial deployment and handoff**
   - Hosted production deployment under the approved domain or subdomain.
   - One client account or authorized-user setup, subject to the final access design.
   - Launch verification of the website, intake routing, workspace, downloads, and
     customer-visible branding.
   - Basic operating handoff covering login, search, analysis, and downloads.

## 3. Not Included in Phase 1

The following require a separate written quote or change order:

- More than the four markets listed in Section 2.
- A promise of ten markets or every lane in any market.
- Skip tracing, phone numbers, personal email addresses, or contact-data licensing.
- Automated outreach, calling, texting, or email campaigns.
- Custom mobile applications.
- New county-source research outside the approved four markets.
- Custom features beyond the existing website and Property Decision Tool capabilities.
- Unlimited support, unlimited revisions, or permanent hosting at no recurring charge.
- Bulk transfer of county-sized source databases or LeadCurate's internal raw files.
- Legal, tax, lending, appraisal, brokerage, or investment advice.

## 4. Expansion

Every additional market is priced and approved separately. Before an expansion is
accepted, LeadCurate will identify:

- County and state.
- Requested lanes.
- Official-source availability and source freshness.
- Expected refresh cadence.
- Required processing, verification, storage, and support work.
- Delivery method and customer access.
- One-time expansion price and any change to the recurring service fee.

No additional market is promised until that review is complete.

## 6. Data and Delivery Rules

- Each market and lane uses the newest official source available at processing time.
- Source date, retrieval date, and source URL are recorded where available.
- Every released lane must pass the LeadCurate quality gate on the exact file made
  available to the client.
- Records are deduplicated to one row per parcel unless the delivery explicitly
  describes another grain.
- Unsupported or failed lanes are marked unavailable or held.
- The workspace uses server-side search and pagination. It does not load an entire
  county into the browser.
- Bulk delivery, when approved, is produced as a controlled CSV or XLSX export rather
  than exposing the underlying raw database.
