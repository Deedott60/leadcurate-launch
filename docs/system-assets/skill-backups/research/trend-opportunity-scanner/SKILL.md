# Trend Opportunity Scanner

Use when Derrick wants to know whether a trend is just noise or a real early opportunity.

## What to scan
- visible buyer requests
- repeated comments asking for a service/product
- creators or pages proving attention
- monetization paths, not just views
- signs a format can become a repeatable workflow

## Output
For each opportunity:
- what the trend is
- who is already paying or likely to pay
- what part is trend noise vs durable value
- how crowded it feels
- fastest small test
- kill condition

## Rules
- Do not confuse traffic with revenue
- Do not recommend a trend if monetization is vague
- Look for asymmetric advantage: taste, timing, tooling, distribution, or local edge
