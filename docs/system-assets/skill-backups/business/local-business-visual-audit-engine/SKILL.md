# Local Business Visual Audit Engine

## Overview

This workflow creates a repeatable, industry-agnostic offer for local businesses: collect lawful/public business data, analyze revenue leaks, produce a high-quality visual report, then sell a practical automation or marketing fix.

The goal is fast monetization without building a huge SaaS first.

## Core offer

> We find where your local business is leaking calls, reviews, bookings, visibility, and follow-up — then install simple automations to recover the money.

Primary deliverable:

- visual audit/report
- scored opportunity list
- competitor comparison
- recommended quick wins
- one installed automation or campaign

## Universal workflow

1. **Choose niche + city**
   - plumbers, roofers, med spas, dentists, restaurants, gyms, lawyers, contractors, detailers, cleaners, etc.

2. **Collect business/market data**
   - Google Business Profile data where allowed/available
   - business website
   - reviews and ratings
   - hours, phone, booking links
   - competitor listings
   - local search result position
   - website screenshots
   - page speed/mobile issues
   - ad/offer examples when visible

3. **Score revenue leaks**
   - missed call risk
   - weak reviews/reply gaps
   - no booking funnel
   - poor mobile site
   - no lead magnet/offer
   - slow follow-up
   - weak local SEO/category setup
   - poor before/after/social proof

4. **Produce high-quality visual report**
   - branded HTML/PDF dashboard
   - screenshots of website/GBP/competitors
   - scorecards and charts
   - specific recommendations
   - estimated impact language without guaranteeing revenue

5. **Create personalized outreach**
   - short email/DM
   - call script
   - Loom/video script
   - SMS-style follow-up if appropriate and compliant
   - one-page proposal

6. **Sell quick implementation**
   - missed-call text-back
   - review request flow
   - lead capture form + instant follow-up
   - reactivation campaign
   - booking funnel
   - Google Business Profile improvement checklist
   - simple landing page

7. **Upsell monthly**
   - dashboard/reporting
   - review management
   - CRM/follow-up automation
   - content repurposing
   - ad landing pages
   - local SEO monitoring

## Recommended tools

- GitHub: source of truth and reusable templates
- Python: data cleaning, scoring, report generation
- Playwright/browser tools: screenshots and audits
- Lighthouse/PageSpeed: website performance
- Google Business Profile/manual export/DataForSEO/SerpAPI where allowed: local search and listings
- OpenStreetMap/OSRM: location/geography when needed
- Supabase/Postgres or Airtable: lead/audit database
- n8n: workflow orchestration
- GoHighLevel/HubSpot/Close/Airtable: CRM depending on client
- Resend/Mailgun/SendGrid: email delivery
- Stripe: payment links/deposits
- Vercel/Cloudflare Pages: report and landing-page hosting
- Canva/HTML/SVG/PowerPoint: visual report/export
- TTS/video tools when available: personalized video audits

## Fast money package ladder

- **Free/low-cost teaser:** 3-minute visual audit screenshot + one obvious leak.
- **Paid audit:** $97–$297 for full Revenue Leak Report.
- **Quick fix:** $497–$1,500 to install one automation/funnel.
- **Monthly:** $500–$2,500/mo for automation, reporting, review/reputation, and campaign support.

- auto detailers/body shops

## Compliance and ethics

Do not scrape behind logins, bypass anti-bot systems, or violate access controls. Use public data, APIs, manual review, exports, or approved tools. Avoid guaranteed revenue claims. Phrase estimates as opportunities, not promises.

## Proof requirements

For every client/prospect deliverable, provide:

- exact business name
- date reviewed
- screenshots or data sources
- score reasoning
- recommended fix
- proposed next action

## Repeatable deliverables

- `Revenue Leak Report.html/pdf`
- `Local Competitor Snapshot`
- `Follow-Up Automation Plan`
- `Review Recovery Plan`
- `Booking Funnel Sketch`
- `Outreach Script + Email`
- `Implementation Checklist`
