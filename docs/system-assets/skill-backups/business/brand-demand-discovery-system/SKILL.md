# Brand Demand Discovery System

Use when Derrick wants to know if people actually want a product/service before building branding, ads, or content.

## Core promise
Before telling a business to spend on branding, content, or commercials, first validate demand:
- Are people already searching for this?
- Are people already asking for it on social media?
- Are competitors getting visible traction?
- Are there intent signals on Facebook, X, TikTok, Reddit, local groups, or Google?

## Workflow
1. Define the offer in one sentence.
2. List the exact customer phrases someone would type or post.
3. Search for intent across:
   - Google
   - X
   - Facebook groups/pages/comments
   - TikTok search
   - Reddit
   - local directories/forums if relevant
4. Separate results into:
   - explicit demand
   - soft curiosity
   - competitor saturation
   - no-signal / weak-signal
5. Decide the lane:
   - strong demand, push hard
   - moderate demand, niche down
   - weak demand, reframe offer
   - no demand, do not force branding spend

## Deliverable
Return:
- demand summary
- where demand is visible
- best audience wording
- best platform to fish for first buyers
- whether the business should spend on branding/commercials yet
- recommended next move

## Rule
Do not assume a product is good just because it looks good. Validate real intent first.
