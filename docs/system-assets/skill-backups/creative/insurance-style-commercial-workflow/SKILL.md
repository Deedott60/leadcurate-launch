# Insurance-Style Commercial Workflow

Use when a user wants to recreate the style of a calm trust-building commercial like the TMA Insurance Trust / Higgsfield reference.

## Style fingerprint
- Human-first, credibility-first
- Real professional in a real office or home-office
- Medium or medium-close framing
- Camera mostly locked or very slow push-in / subtle dolly
- Soft daylight / natural office light
- Burned-in subtitle lines, short and readable
- Calm, measured, non-salesy voice
- Clean white or minimal end card
- No flashy SaaS-demo movement
- No overloaded UI or nightclub flyer energy

## Reusable visual workflow
1. Start with the person in context, not the product.
2. Use 4–6 shots max for a 30–45s cut.
3. Let each shot breathe 4–7 seconds.
4. Prioritize talking head, thoughtful desk work, phone/consultation, and one restrained product/process insert.
5. Keep subtitles short: one sentence or phrase at a time.
6. End on a simple brand lockup, not a loud CTA explosion.

## Shot recipe
1. Establishing office/professional shot
2. Talking-head trust shot
3. Desk/process shot
4. Optional product/process insert
5. Return to professional / reassurance shot
6. Clean end card

## Voice rules
- Warm, grounded, measured
- No hype words, no loud urgency
- Sounds like counsel, not advertising

## Subtitle rules
- Low on frame
- White text on dark band or subtle backing
- 3–8 words per beat when possible
- Match the spoken cadence, do not dump paragraphs

## End card rules
- White or very light background
- Minimal logo lockup
- One restrained brand line
- Optional single CTA line only

## What to avoid
- Hyperactive transitions
- Template motion-flyer energy
- Overuse of product screens
- AI slop backgrounds
- Character inconsistency across scenes
- Fake corporate perfection

## Verification
Before claiming a recreation is on-style, check:
- human office realism
- subtitle treatment
- camera calmness
- trust tone
- simple end card
