## Where to find prospects

### Tier 1 — High signal, low effort (start here)

1. **Facebook groups** — search for your market + "real estate," "wholesaling," "investors," "REI"
   - "Charlotte Real Estate Investors"
   - "[Your city] Wholesalers"
   - "BiggerPockets [city] Group"
   - "[State] REIA" groups
   - Most cities have 5–15 active groups
2. **BiggerPockets forums** — `biggerpockets.com/forums` — look for posts asking about lead sources, list quality, market entry
3. **Local REIA meetups** — most cities have one or two; attendees are buyer-types
4. **Instagram + Twitter hashtag search** — `#wholesaling`, `#offmarket`, `#[city]realestate`, `#flipping`

### Tier 2 — Slower but warmer

5. **LinkedIn** — search "Real Estate Investor" + your target city. Use the "people you may know" warm-intro angle.
6. **Reddit** — `r/realestateinvesting`, `r/wholesaling` — comment helpfully, DM when relevant. Don't spam.
7. **YouTube comment sections** — under wholesaling tutorials, people often ask "where do you get your lists?"

### Tier 3 — Cold but scalable

8. **Skip-trace seller lists you find online** — investor directories, REIA member lists
9. **Cold email to local real-estate offices**

## The outreach rhythm

**Don't blast.** 20–30 thoughtful DMs/SMS per day beats 200 sprayed-and-prayed.

Daily target for the first 2 weeks:
- **15 outbound messages** (DMs + emails)
- **3–5 replies** to active forum threads (warm-up)
- **Add every new prospect to the dashboard** (the "Pipeline" page) so you can track responses
- **Check intake form submissions** (Inbox tab, ring 🔔)

---

## The message ladder

All templates are pre-written in the dashboard's **Templates** tab. Tap to copy.

1. **First message** (SMS / DM) → "SMS — cold intro" template
2. **If they reply** → flip them to "Replied" in the pipeline, then use **"★ Preview + quote"** template — fill in market, count, price, payment method
3. **If they ask "what makes you different"** → "☞ They asked what makes you different" template
4. **If they ask "how much?"** → "☞ They asked how much" template
5. **If they want a sample only** → "☞ They want sample only" template
6. **They agree to pay** → flip to "Quote out," send the payment template ($ Cash App / Zelle / Stripe)
7. **They paid** → flip to "Paid," send "★ Payment received" confirmation, then deliver
8. **You delivered** → flip to "Delivered," send "★ Delivery email" template

Three days after delivery → "⤴ Day-after check-in" + "⤴ Referral ask"
Thirty days later → "⤴ Reactivation" if they've gone quiet

## Compliance reminders (cover yourself)

- **Don't make TCPA/DNC claims you can't back up.** If you're selling address-only, say "address-only, customer responsible for phone compliance." If you're selling phones, you need to either scrub against DNC yourself OR explicitly disclaim it in writing.
- **Don't promise deals.** "Data and education only. Customer handles outreach + closing." Already in the landing page disclaimer.
- **Don't resell or share customer data.** If a buyer sends you their target ZIP list, that's theirs, not yours.
- **State-specific rules vary.** TX, FL, CA have stricter rules than most. When in doubt, address-only + customer-disclaimed.

## If a prospect goes cold

Don't chase forever. Standard rhythm:
- Day 0: initial message
- Day 3: follow-up (template available)
- Day 10: one more nudge
- Day 30: reactivation
- After that: mark "Dead" and move on. They can come back via the intake form anytime.
