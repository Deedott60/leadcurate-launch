# LeadCurate Product Strategy

## Core positioning

LeadCurate is a tech-enabled property intelligence service for serious real estate investors.

We turn messy public-record property data into cleaned, scored, limited-seat lead batches — and include the simple operating tools customers need to work the data without paying thousands for basic scripts.

## Brand promise

**Better data. Cleaner workflow. No hype. Your execution closes the deal.**

## What we sell

We do not sell guaranteed deals, cold outreach, or get-rich training. We sell:

- county/source research
- data cleaning and dedupe
- property-owner classification
- skip-trace enrichment where available
- DNC-status marking and scrub timestamps where applicable
- lead scoring and score reasons
- limited-seat/territory assignment rules
- CSV/XLSX delivery
- practical operator tools

## What we give away / include

LeadCurate should include basic operating tools so customers do not feel forced into a fake academy:

- Seller call prep sheet
- Seller discovery questions
- Follow-up cadence
- Lead batch tracker
- MAO calculator
- Buyer outreach templates
- Title handoff checklist
- Compliance reminder checklist
- First 100 leads action plan

Important: these must be original materials. Do not copy another course's wording, documents, structure, or proprietary training materials.

## Simplified territory model

Every county has a limited seat count based on actual public-record volume and operational reliability.

- **1-seat county:** only one customer can buy that county/lane.
- **2-seat county:** only two customers can buy that county/lane.
- **3-seat county:** only three customers can buy that county/lane.
- **Exclusive territory:** one buyer pays more to block competing seats in the defined county, zone, zip cluster, or lead lane.

Customer-facing wording:

> Some counties are small and only support one customer. Larger counties may support two or three limited seats. Before activation, LeadCurate reviews county volume and tells you how many seats are available. Assigned owner-property records are not resold to another buyer during the active assignment window.

- 4 Operator Seats at $897 = $3,588/month
- 1 Exclusive Territory at $1,497 + 3 County Seats = $2,988/month

The initial goal is 5–7 serious customers, not mass-market sales.

## Enrichment frequency

Do not skip trace every raw record daily. That wastes money.

Recommended flow:

1. Pull public-record data.
2. Clean and dedupe.
3. Remove obvious junk.
4. Score and classify.
5. Approve records for delivery.
6. Skip trace approved records.
7. Mark DNC/status right before delivery.
8. Deliver batch.

Tier schedule:

- County Seat: monthly delivery, optional mid-month update if volume supports it.
- Operator Seat: biweekly target delivery.
- Exclusive Territory: weekly or biweekly depending on source refresh and volume.

## Outreach services

Do not launch as a cold outreach service. That creates too much compliance and operational risk.

Safe launch support:

- scripts
- lead tracker
- follow-up schedule
- CRM/spreadsheet setup
- mail/email templates
- batch prioritization guide
- onboarding call

Later possible partnerships:

- direct mail partner
- CRM setup service
- compliant call center partner
- attorney/compliance-reviewed campaign setup

## AI positioning

Use AI as backend credibility, not hype.

Good wording:

> Automation and AI-assisted workflows help clean, classify, and prioritize public-record property signals faster. Human QA protects quality before delivery.

Avoid:

- AI predicts who will sell
- AI guarantees motivated sellers
- secret AI deal finder
- guaranteed deals
