## Posture that's still valid (carry forward)

These principles from the original strategy remain the LeadCurate operating posture:

- **Process > guarantees.** We sell cleaned, scored, source-attributed data. We don't sell guaranteed deals or guaranteed motivated sellers. The customer still handles outreach, compliance, verification, negotiation, and closing.
- **Transparency > mystery.** Source date included, confidence/distress score included, DNC-status marked where applicable, replacement policy clear.
- **Limited buyer access > mass resale.** 1–3 buyers per market keeps records warm.
- **Education without becoming a course.** Practical training tools included (call prep, follow-up structure, MAO guidance) — never as "free game" YouTube-style framing.
- **Quality-first backend.** Backend is a quality-control system, not just a SaaS form. Preserve raw imports, decision context, exclusion reasons, suppression logic, audit trail.
- **Compliance-aware.** Lawful public sources only. No anti-bot evasion, no IP rotation, no CAPTCHA bypass.
