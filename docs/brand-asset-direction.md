# LeadCurate Brand Asset Direction

## What to borrow from reference landing pages

Use references for structure and taste, not for direct copying.

Good ideas to borrow:

- Shorter county availability form
- Strong repeated CTA language
- Clean benefit cards
- Subtle real estate/property visual cues
- A branded device/tablet image showing how a buyer would use the data

Avoid:

- Generic smiling real estate stock photos
- Overpromising daily delivery
- Saying DNC-clean or safe-to-call
- Fake testimonials
- Low monthly pricing that weakens premium positioning
- Too much course/funnel energy

## Future advertising / hero asset concept

Create a premium field-operator image:

- dark green or emerald tablet/iPad cover
- tablet screen shows a blurred LeadCurate lead batch / spreadsheet
- columns like County, Signal, Source Date, Score, DNC Status, Lead Lane
- background: property exterior, county map, dashboard, or investor desk
- mood: serious, premium, practical, not guru-style
- brand mark: subtle LeadCurate green dot on the tablet cover or screen
- no real owner names, no real addresses, no real leads

Possible use cases:

- landing page supporting visual
- social media ad creative
- LinkedIn post image
- webinar/demo thumbnail
- PDF/operator kit cover

Prompt direction for image generation later:

> Premium editorial product photo of a real estate acquisition operator holding a tablet with a dark emerald green cover. The tablet screen shows a clean blurred spreadsheet-style property lead dashboard with columns for county, source date, urgency score, DNC status, and lead lane. Warm cream and slate color palette, subtle green dot branding, modern residential property and faint parcel map lines in the background, serious data-company aesthetic, no hype, no luxury mansion, no readable private data.
