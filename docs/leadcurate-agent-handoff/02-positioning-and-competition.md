# 02 - Positioning And Competition

## Why this has value even when records are public

Raw public records are not the product.

LeadCurate's product is the reviewed, cleaned, deduped, scored, source-dated, limited-seat workflow.

Buyers can sometimes find raw records themselves, but most do not want to:

- locate the correct county sources
- figure out whether the source is stale or useful
- normalize messy owner and property fields
- dedupe repeated owners/properties
- classify lead lane
- score why a record deserves review
- preserve source dates
- prepare a buyer-ready CSV/XLSX
- repeat the workflow monthly
- avoid buying the same exhausted list as everyone else

## Competitive difference

Many lead sellers compete on mystery and hype. LeadCurate should compete on transparency and operational quality.

LeadCurate says:

> The raw source may be public. The value is the cleaning, dedupe, score context, source date, and limited-seat delivery.

Avoid:

- "secret data"
- "guaranteed motivated sellers"
- "safe to call"
- "nobody else can get this"
- "AI predicts sellers"

Use:

- source-reviewed records
- cleaned and deduped batch
- score reasons
- source dates
- DNC-aware fields where applicable
- limited buyer seats
- assigned-record protection during active access window

## "Free game" strategy

Internally, Derrick describes part of the strategy as giving buyers "the game."

Publicly, use more professional language:

- practical training
- operator tools
- worksheets
- call prep
- follow-up structure
- offer-review guidance
- compliance reminders

The point is to prove that LeadCurate understands investor workflow after delivery, not to become a course company.

## The clean market message

> LeadCurate gives serious buyers cleaner county property data and the practical tools to work it correctly. The data helps. The customer still handles outreach, compliance, verification, negotiation, and closing.
