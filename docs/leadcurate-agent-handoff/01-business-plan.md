# 01 - Business Plan

## Positioning

LeadCurate is a premium county property data operation.

The honest promise:

> Better starting data, clearer context, fewer blind guesses.

The business is not:

- a guaranteed motivated seller product
- a cold calling service
- a legal/compliance advisor
- a course funnel
- a generic stale-list reseller

## Customer

Best early customers:

- solo wholesalers
- small acquisition teams
- buy-and-hold investors
- operators already spending time or money on bad lists
- buyers who understand that execution still matters

Avoid early customers who expect guaranteed deals or want LeadCurate to run their outreach.

## Offer ladder

**County Review Deposit: $175 one-time**

Validates source availability, usable county volume, expected lead lanes, and seat count. Credited toward first month if activated.

**Single Batch: $299 one-time**

One snapshot of a county/source lane where available. Useful as a proof product.

**County Seat: $497/month**

Ongoing limited-seat monthly county batch for a solo operator.

**Operator Seat: $897/month**

Higher volume or two smaller counties, biweekly delivery target where source volume supports it.

**Exclusive Territory: $1,497+/month**

Blocks other buyers from a county, ZIP group, or lead lane under defined rules.

## First revenue target

Target: first $3,000/month.

Paths:

- 6 County Seats at $497 = $2,982/month
- 4 Operator Seats at $897 = $3,588/month
- 2 Exclusive Territories at $1,497 = $2,994/month
- 1 Exclusive Territory plus 3 County Seats = about $2,988/month

## First 30 days

Week 1:

- finalize landing page and legal pages
- set up Supabase intake
- connect form endpoint
- add email alerts
- prepare Stripe links

Week 2:

- select one pilot county
- identify lawful public sources
- pull first source file manually
- preserve raw source file
- run the sample batch script

Week 3:

- refine cleaning, dedupe, score reasons, and sample columns
- choose skip-trace/DNC provider path
- generate 1-2 stronger sample batches

Week 4:

- call local investors and wholesalers
- send sample previews
- sell $175 county review deposits
- use feedback to refine the product
