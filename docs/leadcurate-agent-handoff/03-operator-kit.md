# 03 - Operator Kit

This is the included buyer toolkit. It is not fully built yet, but this is the canonical structure.

## Purpose

Give buyers enough practical guidance to work the batch without making LeadCurate sound like a guru course.

The operator kit differentiates LeadCurate from sellers who only provide a list.

## Included materials to create

### 1. First 100 Leads Action Plan

Purpose: tells the buyer how to review and prioritize the first batch.

Sections:

- how to sort by score
- how to review source date and score reason
- which records to research before calling
- when to mail first instead of call
- how to mark bad data vs no seller motivation

### 2. Seller Call Prep Sheet

Purpose: helps buyer prepare before contacting an owner.

Fields:

- owner name
- property address
- source signal
- score reason
- likely situation
- questions to ask
- red flags
- follow-up date

### 3. Seller Discovery Questions

Purpose: original question bank for investor conversations.

Include:

- property condition
- ownership situation
- timeline
- liens/taxes/repairs
- occupancy
- motivation signals
- preferred next step

Avoid legal advice and aggressive pressure scripts.

### 4. MAO / Offer Review Worksheet

Purpose: helps buyer think through offer range.

Fields:

- estimated ARV
- repair estimate
- desired profit/spread
- closing/holding costs
- assignment/wholesale fee target
- max allowable offer
- notes and assumptions

Important: LeadCurate should not guarantee ARV, repairs, or offer value. This is a worksheet, not valuation advice.

### 5. Follow-Up Cadence

Purpose: simple workflow for follow-up.

Example:

- Day 0: first review/outreach
- Day 2: second touch
- Day 7: follow-up
- Day 14: mail/email touch
- Day 30: nurture review

### 6. Lead Batch Tracker

Columns:

- assignment ID
- lead ID
- owner
- property
- lead lane
- score
- source date
- first touch
- status
- next action
- notes

### 7. Compliance Reminder Checklist

Topics:

- verify public/source record accuracy
- respect DNC/TCPA/state rules
- do not claim LeadCurate says a number is safe to call
- keep records of consent/opt-outs
- stop contact when requested
- customer is responsible for outreach compliance

### 8. Buyer Outreach Templates

Optional templates for buyers who wholesale or assign deals.

Include:

- investor buyer intro
- deal summary format
- property review handoff
- title/company handoff checklist
