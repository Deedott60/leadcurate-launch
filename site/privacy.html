This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information about:

3. Information We Collect From Customers

When you visit the Site or use the Service, we may collect:

  • Account & contact data: email address, business name, target county, buyer profile.
  • Billing data: payment is processed by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

  • Communications: emails, support requests, and form submissions.

4. Information We Collect From Public Records

To produce our curated data files, we collect property and ownership information from:

  • County recorder, assessor, tax-collector, and clerk-of-court public records;
  • Probate and court filings of public record;
  • Third-party data providers and skip-trace vendors who attest their data is sourced legally;
  • Publicly available business registrations and corporate filings.

This information may include property addresses, owner names, mailing addresses, deed and tax history, and, where available, telephone numbers and email addresses associated with the property owner.

10. Your Rights

To exercise any right, email privacy@leadcurate.com. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.
