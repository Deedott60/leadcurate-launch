1. County Review Deposit ($175)

The $175 County Review Deposit is 100% refundable if any of the following are true:

  • We determine the requested county cannot support a viable seat after our review;
  • All available seats in the county have already been filled and we cannot offer you one;
  • You request a refund within 7 days of payment and before we have begun the manual county review.

If we approve the county and you proceed with a subscription, the $175 deposit is credited toward your first month’s invoice and is no longer separately refundable.

2. Single Batch ($299, one-time)

Single Batch files are non-refundable once the curated file has been delivered to you. Because the file is a digital deliverable with permanent commercial value upon receipt, all sales are final after delivery.

If you have not yet received your batch and wish to cancel, contact support@leadcurate.com within 48 hours of purchase for a full refund.

3. Monthly Subscriptions (County Seat, Operator Seat)

Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance. You may cancel at any time, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the then-current billing period. We do not pro-rate or refund unused portions of the current billing month, but you retain access through the end of the period you paid for.

30-Day First-Batch Guarantee: If your first delivered monthly batch fails to meet the standard described on our site (cleaned, deduped, with source dates and applicable DNC flags), notify us within 7 days of delivery. We will, at our option, either re-deliver a corrected batch or refund the first month’s subscription fee. This guarantee applies only to the first monthly batch.

4. Annual Subscriptions

Annual subscriptions are billed in advance for the full 12-month term and are non-refundable after the first 14 days. Within the first 14 days of an annual subscription, you may request a full refund minus the value of any batches already delivered (calculated at the equivalent monthly rate).

5. Exclusive Territory Subscriptions ($1,497+/mo)

Exclusive Territory access is reserved capacity that prevents other buyers from being assigned the same county. Because we hold the territory for you, exclusive-territory fees are non-refundable once the territory is locked. You may cancel at the end of any month with at least 30 days’ written notice.

6. Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer, please contact us at support@leadcurate.com so we can attempt to resolve the issue directly. Chargebacks filed without first contacting us may result in immediate account termination and forfeiture of any remaining access.

7. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@leadcurate.com with the subject line “Refund Request.”
  2. Include your account email, the order ID or invoice number, and the reason for the request.
  3. We respond to all refund requests within 2 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

8. Exceptions

We reserve the right to refuse refunds for accounts that have violated our Terms of Service, engaged in fraud or chargeback abuse, or attempted to download or extract data after a cancellation request.
