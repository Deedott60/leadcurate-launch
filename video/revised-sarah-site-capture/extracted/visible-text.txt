[div] FAQ
[h2] Quick answers before you check a county.
[h3] Why are seats limited?
[p] Because some counties only support a small number of buyers once source volume and usable records are counted.
[h3] What do I get?
[p] A cleaned CSV/XLSX batch with source context, score reasons, assignment details, contact data where available, and DNC-aware fields where applicable.
[h3] Is this exclusive?
[p] Not always. Some counties support one buyer, some support more. The review tells you what is actually possible.
[h3] Do you sell the same records to everyone?
[p] No. Records assigned during an active access period are protected from broad resale.
[h3] Do you guarantee deals?
[p] No. LeadCurate provides data and educational tools. Buyers handle outreach, compliance, verification, negotiation, and closing.
[h3] Is contact data always included?
[p] No. Contact data is included where available, and DNC-aware fields are marked where applicable.
